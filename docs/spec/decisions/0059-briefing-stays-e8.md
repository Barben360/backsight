# 0059 — The session briefing stays the E8 recent-observations list

- **Date**: 2026-10-25
- **Status**: accepted (agent; open to veto)
- **Decided by**: agent, M3 block 3 (0056), from the E14 rerun

## Decision
- SessionStart keeps E8's briefing: the project's 5 most recent observations.
- Per-project mental models are not created, refreshed or injected in v1.

## Why
- In the E14 rerun, a blind judge preferred E8 on 3 of 5 projects, consistently across both orders.
- Every mental model carried statements the newest memories do not support (1–18 per page). A synthesis over all memories reports stale open items as current, and an injected briefing that misleads is worse than an incomplete one.
- A refresh costs about 96 000 input tokens per active project, against none for E8.
