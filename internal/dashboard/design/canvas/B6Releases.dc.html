Comparing two versions
How
Turns are matched by project and by where memory was shown. Each matched pair gives a difference; they are pooled, weighted by size, with a 95 % interval that counts each session once (turns of one session are not independent).
Shown when
Each version has at least 7 days and 30 turns that said what they used in that project and place.
Words
“Differs” when the interval leaves out 0, “no clear difference” otherwise. Never “better”: weeks, work and memory differ.
stats releases --compare A,B · stratified, clustered by session
CLI
backsight stats releases --compare v0.1.16-m7,v0.1.17-m7
Observational, not an experiment. The two versions ran on different weeks, on different work, with different memory. Matching by project and place removes some of that, not all.
Settings differ: recall.min_score was 0.5 under v0.1.16-m7 and 0.55 under v0.1.17-m7 (changed Oct 20). A difference may come from the setting, not the release.
