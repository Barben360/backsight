Press g, then the letter within a second. Keys do nothing while you type in a field. Turn shortcuts off in Preferences.

  • A real <dialog> opened with showModal(): focus moves to Close, Tab stays inside, Esc closes it and focus returns to where it was.

  • Single keys are ignored when Ctrl, Alt or Meta is held, and in any field, so typing and screen reader keys are never taken.
